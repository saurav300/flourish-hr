Policy & Contract Development

Clear employment documentation protects both the employer and employees. We assist businesses with developing compliant HR documentation.

Includes

  • Custom HR policies and workplace procedures
  • Employment contracts (permanent, fixed-term, independent contractors, remote working)

Employment legislation in South Africa requires businesses to maintain strict compliance across all areas of the employment relationship.

Includes

  • Employment Equity planning and submissions
  • Workplace Skills Plans (WSP) and Annual Training Reports (ATR)
  • HR compliance audits
  • Labour legislation compliance guidance

Industrial Relations & CCMA Support

Workplace disputes require professional handling to protect both the organisation and its employees.

Includes

  • Employer representation at the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA)
  • Disciplinary and grievance management
  • Workplace investigations
  • Conflict resolution and dispute management
